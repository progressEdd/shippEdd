The vendored `.agents/skills/shippEdd/` copy picks these changes up via plan 03-04's squash-merge refresh — do not touch anything outside your worktree.

**Composition with 03-01…03-03 (all landed before you fork):** the exact-text contracts below assume their edits are in.
